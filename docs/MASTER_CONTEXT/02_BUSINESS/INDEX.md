# Business Knowledge Domain — INDEX

## Purpose

Documents the business model, economic engine, value streams, stakeholders, and the commercial context that shapes Oship's technical priorities.

## Knowledge Scope

Covers business model canvas, revenue streams, cost structure, key metrics, stakeholders, value streams, and business risk. Interfaces with product (01) and users (03) but does not define technical architecture.

## Responsibilities

The owners of this domain are responsible for:

- Maintain the business model and value stream mapping
- Document revenue and cost structures
- Track business KPIs and commercial metrics
- Record stakeholder and partnership landscape
- Align business objectives with product and roadmap
